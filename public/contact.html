Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for an authorized seed dealership?

Select "Dealership" in the enquiry form above or call our commercial desk at +91 9949003498. Our regional sales team will verify your seed license and share commercial trade terms.

What is the minimum germination percentage on Taj Seeds bags?

We guarantee a minimum of 98%+ germination and genetic purity across all hybrid maize and sorghum varieties, certified by our accredited QC testing laboratory.

Do you deliver seed consignments across India?

Yes. With our centralized dispatch warehouse in Medchal, Hyderabad, we fulfill full-truckload (FTL) and part-truckload (PTL) seed dispatches to over 15 Indian states including Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh.
