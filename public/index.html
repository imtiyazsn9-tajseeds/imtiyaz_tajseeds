Our Journey of Trust & Innovation

From a humble seeds counter in rural Bihar to a high-capacity modern processing facility in Telangana, our journey is rooted in farmers' prosperity.

1980

The Foundation: 'Janta Beej Bhandar'

Founder Mr. Abdus Salam, after 15 dedicated years of selling seeds in rural haat-bazaars, opened 'Janta Beej Bhandar' in a small town in Bihar - a humble shop that grew into an enduring symbol of farmers' trust and genuine agricultural goodwill.

2015

A Modern Vision: 'Taj Seeds'

His son Md. Naushad established 'Taj Seeds' with a modern technological vision. The enterprise was named in loving honor of his brother Md. Mumtaj, reflecting deep family unity, ethics, and a shared dedication to transforming Indian agriculture.

Today

Pan-India Reach & Medchal Hub

Today, Taj Seeds operates its corporate Head Office and an expansive, state-of-the-art conditioning warehouse in Medchal, Hyderabad (Telangana), delivering premium seeds across India.
