About Us

Four decades of unwavering commitment to Indian agriculture, family dedication, and farmer prosperity.

Rooted in Soil, Driven by Trust

The story of Taj Seeds Agri Sciences began in 1980 with our visionary founder, Mr. Abdus Salam. Having spent 15 dedicated years travelling across rural haat-bazaars understanding the daily hardships, soil challenges, and hopes of smallholder farmers, Mr. Abdus Salam founded 'Janta Beej Bhandar' in Kishanganj, Bihar.

In 2015, his son Md. Naushad established 'Taj Seeds' with an ambition to merge this deep-rooted generational trust with modern agricultural biotechnology. The enterprise was named in loving honor of his brother Md. Mumtaj, embodying an enduring ethos of family solidarity, ethics, and collective responsibility toward the Indian farmer.

"True to our motto 'Every Step with Farmer', we do not simply sell seeds; we walk alongside the farmer from sowing to harvest."
