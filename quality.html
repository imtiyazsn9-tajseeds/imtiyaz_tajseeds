The 5-Stage Seed Quality Pipeline

From breeder isolation fields to farmer field dispatch, each bag passes through rigorous scientific checks.

1

Seed Production

Multi-stage field roguing, strict parental isolation distance, and monitored detasseling under expert breeders.

2

Processing & Cleaning

Multi-screen air conditioning, gravity separation, and indent cylinder sizing removing chaff, broken kernels, and inert matter.

3

Quality Testing

Standard germination incubators, moisture analyzer checks (<11%), seed vigor index, and genetic purity verification.

4

Protective Packing

Fungicide/polymer seed dressing, electronic high-accuracy weighing, and heat-sealed moisture-barrier bagging.

5

Delivery to Farmers

Climate-safe logistics from Medchal Hyderabad warehouse reaching agricultural dealers and farming families on schedule.

Modern Seed Cleaning & Gravity Separation

Our processing plant in Medchal is equipped with cutting-edge multi-deck air-screen cleaners and densimetric gravity separators. This machinery separates grains based on exact terminal velocity and specific weight, discarding immature or insect-damaged seeds.

  • Achieves 99.8% mechanical purity across commercial lots
  • Continuous dust aspiration to protect seed embryo vitality

Certified Seed Testing & Quality Plant Operations

Our in-house quality control laboratory operates under stringent protocols aligned with Indian Minimum Seed Certification Standards. Each seed lot is subjected to standard paper towel and sand germination evaluations, moisture testing, and seed health profiling.

  • Only lots achieving 98%+ germination are released for packaging
  • Electronic moisture analyzers ensuring optimum equilibrium moisture content

Automated Moisture-Proof Bagging System

Proper packaging is essential to shield high-vigor hybrid seeds from ambient humidity and pest infestation during transport and village storage. We utilize multi-layer laminated barrier bags with tamper-proof security stitching and verifiable lot numbers.

  • High-speed computerized weighment with 0.1% accuracy
  • Polymer seed treatment enhancing early seedling emergence and drought start

Medchal Central Storage & Nationwide Logistics

Our warehouse in Medchal, Hyderabad spans thousands of square feet of insulated, ventilated, and pest-proof storage space. Organized on heavy-duty pallet racks, our seed inventory is constantly tracked via automated warehouse management software.

  • Rapid dispatch turnarounds to dealers and agricultural cooperatives
  • Buffer stocks maintained to prevent farmer shortages during peak sowing seasons

Require Seed Quality Certifications or Test Reports?

We provide lot-wise purity certificates, germination test reports, and moisture profiles for government tenders, commercial farming corporations, and agricultural dealers.
